SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Services Agreement is between Harbor Street LLC ("Customer") and Northline Data LLC ("Vendor").
It is fiction and not a form.

1. Term
This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues for one year. Either party may terminate for convenience on thirty (30) days' prior written notice.

2. Suspension
Vendor may suspend service on ten (10) business days' notice if an invoice is unpaid.

3. Emergency
Vendor may suspend immediately, without prior notice, if a security incident is underway. That sentence has no day count.

4. Renewal
The term renews for successive one-year periods unless Customer gives 60 days' notice before the renewal date.

5. Assignment
Neither party may assign this Agreement except to a successor, and the notice of assignment must go out 5 days' prior written notice after the transfer.

No other notice window is stated. A 90-day cure is not a notice window unless the word notice appears with a day count.
